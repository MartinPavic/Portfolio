## Overview

ShipLog reads the commits, merged pull requests, closed issues and releases of your repositories and uses an AI model to write a ship log: a weekly update, changelog, standup, client report, release notes or investor update.

## Features

- Projects group the repositories behind one log, with a template, tone, audience and custom instructions
- Streaming generation with OpenAI, Anthropic or Grok, using your own key or managed AI on the paid plan
- Per-project history with search, pinned logs, version history and in-place regeneration
- Read-only share links (optionally password protected) and a public changelog
- Exports to Markdown, HTML and Slack, plus delivery to Slack, Discord and email on a weekly schedule
- Teams through Clerk Organizations, and Stripe subscriptions with a customer portal

## Engineering notes

- Plan limits live in one module, and billing can be switched off with a single flag
- A guided setup script creates the environment file, generates secrets, checks the database and applies migrations
- Full documentation covers architecture, service setup, configuration and deployment
